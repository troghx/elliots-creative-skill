# Reference notes — observed 2026-09-04

These are bounded observations and transferable design interpretations, not template requirements. Refresh a reference when matching its current details. Read only the relevant section.

## Cofounder — https://cofounder.co/

Observed in the public homepage: a blue pixel-art landscape with a laptop anchors the opening; a translucent navigation and task-status fragments share that scene. Below it, pale surfaces, quieter typography and product-interface previews explain the product. This was a homepage review, not an audit of the authenticated application.

Transfer: give the product a recognizable character while keeping the work itself calm. Distinguish invitation, explanation and operation with deliberate hierarchy. A strong image can establish a world; it does not need to repeat behind every field.

For a repeat-use app, that lesson may become consistent material, a distinctive control or a carefully chosen empty state. Do not import the marketing hero, pixel art, social proof, autonomous-agent claims or expansive spacing by default.

## Gooey — https://libraries.dev/gooey

Observed public playground: an expandable central control reveals related file/image/folder actions. The visible demo offers Morph, Move, Bend and Melt plus blur and contrast controls. The page describes liquid surfaces with crisp text; opening the menu was tested in the browser. The visible usage example identifies `liquid-gooey` and `Liquid`, but no package was installed or performance-certified in this review.

Transfer: animate the relation between controls rather than animate unrelated decoration. Separate surface distortion from text/icon rendering. Opening and closing must communicate the same spatial relationship, and repeated input must not trap focus or produce duplicate actions.

Possible fits: contextual quick actions or an optional playful toggle. Poor fits: deforming money values, moving a target during confirmation, or making a frequent action wait for an animation. The reference does not require React or this dependency. Before actual integration, check current API, license, accessibility and reduced-motion support in the real project.

## Jiro — https://jiro.build/components

Observed public catalog and thumbnails: featured compositions range from software directories and calculators to commerce and personal brands; component families recur across headers, features and supporting sections. Review was limited to public catalog visuals, not premium source code or all interactive states.

Transfer: use a consistent family of proportions, type, imagery and controls. For app redesigns, inspect UI-relevant examples and learn hierarchy rather than transplanting a landing-page block. A calculator promo is not evidence of a usable everyday calculator.

No fixed catalog counts are needed. Do not bypass premium access, purchase anything without authorization or copy unlicensed assets. Existing historical Jiro notes in the original creative skill are dated and optional, not a requirement to use their hero patterns.
