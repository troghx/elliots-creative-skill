# Elliot's Creative

Find a clear idea worth building. Let the medium serve it.

Start from audience, intended action, content and actual constraints. Explore enough to find a strong direction, not a fixed number of alternatives. A new utility still needs utility; a campaign can be more theatrical.

Develop a coherent visual language through composition, type, color, imagery and interaction. Avoid copying a fashionable bundle of effects. Boldness may be structural, tactile or quiet; it is not a required hero size.

Hugeicons is the main icon reference. Lucide is a welcome alternative. Verify exports and licensing; maintain coherent stroke, scale and alignment within each area. No forced icon migration for a small edit.

Prototype the defining moment, then make the whole flow work. Keep genuine product behavior, legibility, keyboard access, error states and reduced motion intact. Verify the actual rendered result before calling it finished.
