# Intake

The interview decides the design more than any reference does. Ask what changes the outcome, nothing else.

## Rules

- Read the evidence first: repo, running app, URL, screenshots, brand files. Never ask what the code or page already answers.
- One round of 3–6 questions. Add a second round only if an answer opened a real fork.
- Ask in the user's language. Offer choices with a sensible default ("Si no sabes, asumo X"). Use the client's question tool when available.
- Never invent proof for the user: testimonials, logos, metrics and prices come from them or stay as clearly marked placeholders.
- Small, already-specified fix: skip the interview.

## Universal questions

Pick the ones the evidence leaves open.

1. **What is it?** Tool you or your team use daily · dashboard or internal app · AI/agent interface · landing page that sells · marketing site or campaign · portfolio or personal brand · other.
2. **New or existing?** For existing: what must stay (functions, data, URLs, SEO, brand pieces), and how much may change (reskin · re-layout · rethink)?
3. **Who and how often?** One person all day, a team weekly, strangers once?
4. **The one action:** finish a task, buy, sign up, book, contact, explore, share.
5. **Tone:** three words, plus one thing they never want to see.
6. **Constraints:** stack, devices, light/dark, deadline, budget for paid parts (BoardUI Pro, fonts, stock).

## Surface-specific follow-ups

**Repeat-use tool / internal app**
- The three most frequent tasks, in order.
- What must be visible together to decide or act?
- Dense and fast, or roomy and calm? Keyboard shortcuts wanted?
- What annoys them about the current version?

**Dashboard / analytics**
- What decision does it support, and who makes it?
- The 3–5 numbers that matter; compared against what (last period, target, peers)?
- Where does the data come from and how much of it is there?

**AI / agent interface**
- What does the agent do, and how long does a task run?
- What must the user approve, edit or be able to stop?
- What should be visible while it works (steps, sources, cost, limits)?

**Landing page that sells**
- What is sold, at what price, to whom?
- The single call to action and what happens after the click.
- Proof they actually have (customers, numbers, press, demos) and the main objection buyers raise.
- Where traffic comes from (ads, social, search, direct link).

**Marketing site / campaign**
- The one message and the date or moment it serves.
- What should people remember or share afterwards?
- Assets available: photography, video, illustration, 3D, mascot.

**Portfolio / personal brand**
- Who should hire or contact them?
- The 3–5 strongest works and what each proves.
- How much personality: quiet professional · confident · openly playful?

## Brief template

Write this back before proposing directions.

```text
Surface: <type> · <new | redesign: reskin / re-layout / rethink>
For: <who>, <how often>; the one action is <action>
Must keep: <functions, data, brand, URLs>
Tone: <three words>; avoid <thing>
Constraints: <stack, devices, budget, deadline>; BoardUI: <install | reference only | Pro allowed>
```

If the user skipped the questions, mark inferred lines with "(supuesto)".
